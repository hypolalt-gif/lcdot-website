About the Department

The Department of Transportation is a department responsible for managing and maintaining transportation systems. DOT personnel handle duties such as traffic control, road maintenance, and vehicle towing when applicable. The department responds to roadway incidents, assists other emergency services, and manages construction or road closure operations.

Our Divisions

Review the divisions below. Each has specific requirements and duties. Click "Apply" to open the respective Google Form.

Field Training Bureau (FTO)

The Field Training Bureau focuses on training new members, such as Probationary Operators. Their mission is to make sure the Department of Transportation has well-trained operators.

Apply for FTO

Heavy Operations (HOD)

The Heavy Operations division focuses on complex operations involving heavy machinery. They manage tasks such as lifting a vehicle out of water, including rivers and lakes. Their mission is to operate heavy machinery with care and safety.

Apply for HOD

Office of Operator Standards

The Office of Operator Standards focuses on managing complaints made by civilians or by members of the Department of Transportation. Their mission is to handle every report with care and responsibility.

Handpicked / Interview Only

Construction Operations (COD)

The Construction Operations Division focuses on keeping the roads maintained and up to safety standards. They are tasked with creating new roads, repairing roads, and working on projects for the city.

Apply for COD

Community Coordination (CCD)

The Community Coordination Division is a highly specialized division in LKDOT providing the department with mass shifts, newsletters, important news, and more! To apply, review requirements below!

Apply for CCD
