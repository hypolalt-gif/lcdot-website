Our Mission

The Lakeview City Department of Transportation is dedicated to providing safe, efficient, and reliable transportation infrastructure for all citizens. Our operators and supervisors work tirelessly to maintain the roads and respond to emergencies.

Our Divisions

Field Training (FTO)

Ensuring every new operator meets the highest standards of safety and professionalism before entering the field.

Heavy Operations (HOD)

Our specialized heavy wrecker and recovery team, handling the most complex incidents on the highway.

Construction (COD)

Managing long-term infrastructure projects, lane closures, and specialized construction operations.

Community Coordination (CCD)

Working with the public and other agencies to ensure events and community needs are met safely.
