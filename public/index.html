About the Department

The Lakeview City Department of Transportation (DOT) is responsible for managing and maintaining transportation systems. Our personnel handle traffic control, road maintenance, and vehicle towing operations. We respond to roadway incidents, assist emergency services, and manage city-wide construction projects.

Our Divisions

Field Training (FTO)

The Field Training Bureau focuses on training new members, such as Probationary Operators, to ensure all personnel meet department standards.

Heavy Operations (HOD)

Focusing on complex operations involving heavy machinery, such as lifting vehicles out of water, performed with care and safety.

Construction (COD)

Keeping roads maintained and up to safety standards. Tasked with creating new roads, repairing existing ones, and city projects.
